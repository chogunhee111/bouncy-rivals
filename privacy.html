Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-24

Shorts Factory Studio ("the app") is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the Bouncy Rivals YouTube channel (youtube.com/@bouncyrivals) to produce and publish original animated videos on that channel. It is not offered to other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google: http://www.google.com/policies/privacy

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app accesses:

  • the owner's channel and uploaded videos (to upload videos and thumbnails and to check their status);
  • statistics and YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's own channel;
  • public comments posted on the owner's own videos (to find viewer matchup requests);
  • public YouTube search results for generic niche keywords.

The app does not access any other Google account and does not collect names, contact details, passwords or any other personal information of anyone.

How the information is used, stored and shared

All data is stored only on the owner's computer and is never sold or shared. YouTube API data is never sent to anyone. Retention:

  • OAuth token: until access is revoked; deleted immediately on disconnect.
  • Statistics and analytics of the owner's videos, and scores derived from them: at most 36 months; authorization is re-verified at least every 30 days.
  • Video status information: refreshed at least every 30 days.
  • Comments: comment text is not stored. The comment ID, a link to the comment and the commenter's public display name are kept with a viewer request for at most 30 days unless re-verified with YouTube. Commenters' names are never shown in videos, titles or descriptions.
  • Public search results (other videos' titles, channel names, view counts): deleted after at most 30 days.
  • Logs: deleted after 30 days.

Ads, cookies and tracking

The app shows no ads and allows no third party to serve content or ads. The app does not store or read cookies or similar technologies on any user's device, other than the local Google sign-in token described above. This website sets no cookies.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in the app ("Disconnect & delete YouTube data"), which deletes all stored YouTube API data immediately, or via the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. If access is revoked there, all stored YouTube API data is deleted within 30 days. Deletion requests sent to the contact address below are completed within 7 days.

Instagram and TikTok (optional cross-posting)

If the owner connects them, the app also publishes the owner's own Shorts to the owner's own Instagram professional account (Instagram Graph API) and sends them as drafts to the owner's own TikTok account (TikTok Content Posting API, upload/inbox mode; the owner reviews and posts each draft in the TikTok app).

  • Data used: the owner's account ID and username, and access/refresh tokens, stored only on the owner's computer in files readable only by the owner's Windows account.
  • Data sent: the owner's own video file and a caption built from the owner's own title, description and tags. No YouTube API data (no comments, commenter names or statistics) is sent to Meta or TikTok.
  • Nothing is read from other people's Instagram or TikTok accounts. Posting status (post IDs, times, errors) is kept in the owner's local files.
  • Disconnecting deletes the stored token (and for TikTok asks TikTok to revoke it). Access can also be removed in Instagram Settings → Apps and websites, or in TikTok Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Data deletion requests: email the contact address below; all stored Instagram / TikTok data is deleted within 7 days. Content already posted stays on the owner's accounts until the owner deletes it there.
