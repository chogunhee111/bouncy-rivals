Shorts Factory Studio

Shorts Factory Studio is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the Bouncy Rivals YouTube channel. It produces original, procedurally generated animated videos (cartoon battle simulations, marble races and color wars) and helps publish them on the owner's own channel.

It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
